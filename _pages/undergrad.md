I enjoy helping undergraduate researchers turn an initial idea into a working experiment or prototype. Depending on the project, that may mean planning a sensing setup, learning to work safely in the laboratory, collecting/organising data, testing early designs, or preparing a poster and manuscript.

## Selected Mentorship Activities

**Undergraduate Research Course Projects**

Through RES 201, I am working with an undergraduate researcher developing and testing LiDAR-based sensing prototypes for cognitive-city building-safety applications. 

The resulting work is being developed into manuscripts for submission to the KFUPM Journal of Undergraduate Research and Innovation (JURI).

**KFUPM Inbound Summer Research Program 2026**

I supervised an undergraduate visiting researcher during the KFUPM Inbound Summer Research Program 2026. The six-week project used a multimode-fiber specklegram sensing setup.

The mentorship included experimental planning, safe operation of the optical setup, laboratory safety practices, data collection, storage and processing, and development of machine-learning models for data analysis. I also guided the preparation of a research poster and journal manuscript.

The project was presented at the programme’s research showcase. A manuscript based on the work is currently being prepared for submission to JURI.

**KFUPM Summer Internship Program**

I supervised an undergraduate intern conducting fiber Bragg grating (FBG) sensor experiments for underwater temperature monitoring.

The work included training in laboratory safety, optical-equipment operation, experimental setup, data collection, and analysis. The project resulted in a conference-paper submission, with an additional manuscript currently being prepared for submission to JURI.
