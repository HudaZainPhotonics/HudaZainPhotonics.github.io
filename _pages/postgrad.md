## Mentorship at KFUPM

At King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals, I mentored a Master’s researcher throughout her laboratory-based research project from May 2025 to May 2026.

My support included training on fiber Bragg grating experimental setups. And I provided her with training on the safe operation/use of laboratory equipment, guidance on the design of optical components, support for experimental planning and laboratory execution, and feedback on thesis writing.


## Mentorship at the University of Malaya

At the University of Malaya, I mentored Master’s students working with fiber-tapering machines, tunable laser sources, amplified spontaneous emission (ASE) sources, optical spectrum analysers, and related optical-fiber sensing equipment.

The mentorship included laboratory safety, correct equipment operation, optical alignment, experimental planning, measurement collection, and troubleshooting during fiber-optic experiments.

I also mentored PhD researchers working on D-shaped fiber fabrication and microresonator sensing platforms. This included guidance on the use of fiber-tapering and fusion-splicing equipment.

## Surface-Plasmon-Resonance Sensing

I mentored a postgraduate researcher developing and operating a surface-plasmon-resonance (SPR) sensing setup based on the Kretschmann configuration.

The mentorship covered optical-component selection, open-space optical alignment, safe operation of lasers and optical equipment, experimental design, and the practical challenges of building a stable SPR measurement platform.
