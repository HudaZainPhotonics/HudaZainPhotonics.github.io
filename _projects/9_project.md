## Overview

This ongoing project explores a compact LiDAR-based prototype for safety monitoring in cognitive-city and smart-built-environment applications.

## My Role

I supervised the undergraduate research student throughout the project as part of the RES 201 research course at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM).

My role included guidance on the sensing concept, prototype development, experimental planning, data interpretation, technical writing, and refinement of the research direction.

## Outcomes

The work produced a functioning experimental prototype.

A research manuscript based on the project is currently being prepared for submission to the KFUPM Journal of Undergraduate Research and Innovation (JURI).

## Status

Ongoing and unpublished research. Detailed system design, sensing logic, experimental conditions, and quantitative results are not currently posted.
