## Overview

This project develops a fiber-optic distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) approach for monitoring oil-and-gas pipeline infrastructure in harsh operating environments. The work investigates how acoustic events measured along an optical fiber can support earlier and more reliable identification of pipeline-related activity.

## Funding

## My Role

## Experimental Platform

A controlled laboratory pipeline-model platform is used to generate and analyse representative acoustic events. This enables repeatable evaluation of signal-processing methods before future field-scale deployment.

## Expected Outcomes

- Improved signal quality in DAS measurements
- Reduced sensitivity to environmental and operational noise
- A validated laboratory workflow for fiber-optic pipeline monitoring
- A foundation for lower-false-alarm event detection and future predictive monitoring
