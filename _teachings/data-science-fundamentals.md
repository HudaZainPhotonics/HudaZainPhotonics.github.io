The course covers research types and databases, reading and writing scientific papers and proposals, research methods and literature reviews, time management, critical thinking, and research presentation and dissemination.Students develop the skills needed to identify a research topic, review relevant literature, communicate research ideas clearly, and complete a guided research-based outcome.
