## Course Overview

Digital Systems Laboratory provided practical experience in constructing, testing, and analysing electrical and digital electronic circuits. The laboratory complemented electrical engineering coursework through hands-on activities involving circuit components, breadboards, logic circuits, measurement, circuit analysis, and prototype development.
